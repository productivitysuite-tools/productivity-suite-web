Frequently asked questions

Clear answers before you install.

What the extensions do, where your data stays and how Pro works.

Where is my data stored?

Packs, profiles, settings and local activity records stay in Chrome storage on your device. Productivity Suite does not operate a server that receives their contents.

Do the extensions submit or publish anything for me?

No. AutoFill Pro never submits a form, and Research Packs never publishes your research. You review and approve every final action.

How do I activate Pro?

Install the extension, open its settings and go to License. The Upgrade to Pro button opens the secure ExtensionPay checkout and the extension refreshes your licence afterwards.

Can I use the extensions for free?

Yes. AutoFill includes 10 fills per day and Research Packs includes up to 10 saved packs. The comparison on each product page shows exactly what Pro adds.

Does the website use analytics or an email newsletter?

No. This website does not use analytics, advertising cookies or a newsletter form. The support email is only used when you decide to contact us.

What happens if I uninstall an extension?

Chrome removes its local extension storage. Export a JSON backup first if you want to keep your packs or profiles.
